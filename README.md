ResusPro BloodGas 

A web-based point-of-care blood gas and chemistry simulator designed for medical education, simulation, and assessment.

Purpose

ResusPro BloodGas allows faculty to rapidly generate realistic blood gas analyser printouts without requiring access to a clinical analyser. Results can be customised, saved, printed, exported to PDF, and used as part of simulation scenarios, OSCEs, ALS courses, FIMC training, and debriefing.

Features

- Mobile-friendly interface
- Realistic analyser-style printouts
- Arterial, venous, and capillary sample types
- Configurable blood gas, electrolyte, renal, cardiac, inflammatory, coagulation, and metabolic parameters
- Automatic abnormality flags (↑ / ↓)
- Optional display of units and reference ranges
- Scenario templates and progression gases
- Automatic local history of printed/exported results
- Browser printing and PDF export
- ResusPro branding and logo integration

Typical Use Cases

- ALS and ILS teaching
- FIMC and pre-hospital training
- Emergency Medicine simulation
- Critical care simulation
- ABG interpretation teaching
- DKA, respiratory failure, sepsis, PE, ACS, and other scenario-based training

Privacy

All data is stored locally within the user's browser. No patient data is transmitted to external servers.

Future Development

- QR-linked simulation assets (ECGs, imaging, investigation results)
- Faculty notes and instructor tools

Disclaimer

This software is intended for simulation, education, and training purposes only. It must not be used for clinical decision-making or patient care.
